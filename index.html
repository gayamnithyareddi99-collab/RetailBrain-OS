Core Engine

Economic Decision Graph

Our proprietary GNN-based engine models non-linear interdependencies across SKU, store, and brand levels capturing propagation effects before they occur.

Unlike traditional silos, the EDG captures how a pricing decision in one SKU affects inventory across related brands and labour in neighbouring territories. Built on PyTorch and Deep Graph Library, it processes 1M+ SKU-store combinations per second with sub-500ms decision latency.
Simulation

Counterfactual Retail Twin

Run thousands of "what-if" scenarios in parallel before physical execution. Compare markdown vs. stock transfer vs. labour reallocation with quantified probability scores.

The Economic Twin reduces the 71% failure rate associated with store-level initiatives by simulating outcomes digitally first. GAN-based synthetic scenario generation addresses cold-start problems for new products and unprecedented market shifts.
Proprietary Metric

Explainable Inaction Costing

A unique mathematical framework that quantifies the daily financial penalty of "doing nothing" for every SKU transforming the platform from a dashboard into a commercial necessity.

The EIC algorithm calculates the "shadow loss" of delayed decisions with 92% accuracy. Every alert is backed by transparent economic logic aligned with ICO guidance on AI transparency, solving the "black box" trust gap at the store level.
Security

Zero-Trust Edge Architecture

Operates autonomously within stores when cloud connectivity fails. All commercial logic encrypted and processed locally "blackout-proof" retail intelligence.

Deployed via Docker and Kubernetes on NVIDIA Jetson or Raspberry Pi 4+ edge nodes, the system maintains millisecond-level inference without constant cloud pings. Compliant with PCI DSS, GDPR, and EU AI Act 2024 with end-to-end Zero-Trust encryption.
Learning

Action-Response Memory Loop

A continuous agentic learning engine that tracks every intervention's outcome, refining future recommendations based on real-world execution accuracy.

The reinforcement learning mechanism adapts to seasonal shifts and evolving UK consumer behaviour without requiring manual retraining. The "Speed of Culture" loop ensures the AI remains relevant to changing market conditions automatically.

Store managers receive clear, data-backed directives via React Native mobile interface. Central teams see a Control Tower for group-wide strategic oversight.
